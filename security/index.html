Built for your finance & IT review

Your spend data, handled like the sensitive data it is.

You're sending us invoices, supplier lists and contract rates. Here's exactly how that data is treated, end to end.

Encryption in transit and at rest

Spend exports are encrypted in transit and at rest for the duration they're stored and processed.

Least-privilege access

Access to a customer's data is limited to the people directly working on that account's assessment — not the whole team by default.

Data minimization

We ask for the fields the analysis actually needs — invoices, suppliers, contract rates. Nothing beyond that is required to run an assessment.

Defined retention

Spend data is retained only for as long as needed to deliver the assessment and any agreed follow-up work — see Data Processing for specifics.

Deletion on request

You can request deletion of your spend data at any time once it's no longer needed for active work — contact us and we'll confirm once it's done.

No resale, no model training on your data

Your invoices, suppliers and contract terms are used to deliver your assessment — not sold, and not used to train models for other customers.

What we ask for — and what we don't

Running a Cost Control Assessment needs a standard spend export: invoices, supplier records, and — where you have them — contract rates. We don't need admin access to your ERP, a live integration, or any data beyond what's in that export.

Where your data lives

Spend data is processed to produce your findings report and is not shared with third parties beyond the sub-processors needed to run the service. A full sub-processor list and hosting region details are available on request during a security review.

Evaluating CostWarden for a vendor security review?

Happy to fill out a security questionnaire, sign a mutual NDA, or set up a call with whoever runs procurement or IT security on your side. Reach out via the contact page and we'll get you what you need.
