Questions

Common questions, answered plainly.

How does CostWarden find overcharges in invoices?＋

CostWarden reads every invoice and contract in a company's spend export and cross-checks prices, suppliers and line items against what was actually agreed. It flags patterns like a supplier's price creeping up over time, the same invoice being paid more than once, or billing that has drifted above a contracted rate.

What kind of data do I need to share to get started?＋

A standard spend export from your ERP or accounting system — invoices, suppliers, and where available, contract rates. No integration or IT project is required to run the free assessment.

Is the first assessment really free?＋

Yes. The Cost Control Assessment is free and comes with no commitment. CostWarden analyzes your spend export and shows you where money is likely leaking, with an estimated euro value for each finding.

Which accounting or ERP systems does CostWarden work with?＋

CostWarden works with spend exports from common ERP and accounting systems, including SAP, Oracle and NetSuite, as well as plain spreadsheet exports. No rip-and-replace or long rollout is required.

How is this different from a manual audit?＋

A manual audit samples a subset of invoices because reading everything by hand doesn't scale. CostWarden reads every line of every invoice, so smaller, easy-to-miss overpayments and duplicates are caught alongside larger ones.

How much money can a business typically recover?＋

It depends on the business, but addressable savings from overpayments and billing leakage are commonly estimated in the range of 3% to 8% of annual supplier spend. The free assessment gives an exact figure based on real data rather than an estimate.
