Data Processing

Last updated: [DATE] · Draft pending legal review

This page explains, in plain language, how spend data is processed during a Cost Control Assessment. It is not a substitute for a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — see the note at the bottom to request one.

1. What data this covers

This page covers the spend data you submit specifically to run a Cost Control Assessment or related engagement: invoices, supplier records, and, where available, contract rates. It does not cover general site usage — see our Privacy Policy for that.

2. Roles

For this data, you act as the data controller and CostWarden ([Company legal name]) acts as a data processor, processing spend data only on your instructions and for the purpose of delivering your assessment.

3. Purpose and scope of processing

Spend data is processed solely to: detect potential overpayments, duplicate charges, off-contract billing and price variance; quantify each finding; and produce your findings report. It is not used for any other customer's assessment, and it is not used to train models for other customers.
