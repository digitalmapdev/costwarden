The full walkthrough

No black box. Here's exactly what happens to your data.

Five steps, in plain language — from the file you send us to the euro figure you can act on.

1. Upload — a spend export, nothing else

You send a standard spend export from whatever you already use — SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, or a plain spreadsheet pulled from your accounting system. There's no connector to install, no IT ticket, and no rip-and-replace of your existing tools. If you can export invoices, suppliers and (where you have them) contract rates, you can start.

2. Analyze — every line, not a sample

A manual audit typically samples a subset of invoices, because reading thousands of line items by hand doesn't scale. CostWarden reads every invoice, every supplier record and every contract rate in the export — so the smaller, easy-to-miss issues surface alongside the large ones.

3. Detect — the patterns that actually cost you money

The system cross-checks what you were billed against what you actually agreed to pay, and looks for patterns like:

  • Price creep — a supplier's price for the same item rising gradually, invoice over invoice.
  • Duplicate payments — the same invoice paid more than once, often across different cost centers or locations.
  • Off-contract billing — real invoiced prices drifting above the rate you signed up for.
  • Cross-supplier variance — one supplier charging noticeably more than another for the exact same items.

4. Quantify — a euro figure for every finding

Every issue CostWarden surfaces comes with an estimated euro impact, so your team can prioritize by size instead of digging through a wall of flagged rows to work out what matters.

5. Report — traceable back to the source line

You get a plain-English findings report. Every finding links back to the original invoice line it came from, so your finance and procurement team can verify each one before acting on it — nothing is presented as a black-box score you have to take on faith.

What's shipped today vs. what's coming. Upload, Analyze, Detect, Quantify and Report are live in the Cost Control Assessment. Continuous monitoring, AI-generated fix recommendations, and guided recovery workflows are on the roadmap — see the product roadmap on the homepage for status.

How long does it take?

Most first assessments return findings within two weeks of receiving a usable spend export. There's no software to install on your side while that's happening, and no ongoing time commitment from your team beyond sending the export and reviewing the report.

What if we don't have clean data?

Most spend exports aren't clean, and that's expected. CostWarden is built to work with the export as it exists in your ERP or accounting system today — messy column names, inconsistent supplier naming and multiple locations included.
